# Marketing Campaign Decision Dashboard — draft case study

**Status:** local technical demonstration; synthetic data. Final analyst recommendation and personal contribution statement must be completed after hands-on review.

## Problem and audience

A fictional education business needs to understand marketing acquisition performance and assess a proposed budget. The intended audience is its marketing manager and campaign analyst.

## Approach

Define the acquisition cohort and outcome cutoff; validate four related source files; aggregate spend and outcomes safely in SQL; compare campaign volume and efficiency; explore allocations using historical CAC and explicit customer-value assumptions. A second seeded dataset demonstrates reuse of the same data contract.

## Evidence to collect during your review

Capture the overview, a campaign comparison and the scenario assumptions/results. Explain one data-quality issue, one metric definition, one finding and one recommendation. Record the exact filter window for reproducibility. Do not invent findings before analyzing the output.

## Limitations

Fictional outcomes do not demonstrate achieved business improvement. Historical attribution does not establish incremental sales. Scenarios hold channel CAC constant, ignore saturation and depend on user-supplied economics. Real data would require mapping, validation and business review.

## Contribution and interview preparation

The initial implementation was AI-assisted. Describe your actual work in problem framing, calculation review, dashboard decisions and recommendations. Practise explaining why lead volume alone is insufficient, why order joins can overcount customers, and why a manager needs sensitivity cases before allocating money.
