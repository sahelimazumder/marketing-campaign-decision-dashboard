# First learning checkpoint: frame the decision

## Fictional client

Ranchi Skills Academy is an invented professional education provider. It uses Paid Search, Paid Social, Email and Partner campaigns. Its marketing manager needs to compare lead quality and customer acquisition before proposing the next budget.

## Decision

Which channels deserve a cautious budget increase, which need investigation, and what financial assumptions make that proposal worthwhile?

## Questions

1. How much did we spend, and how many leads, qualified leads and new customers did acquisition cohorts produce?
2. Which campaigns combine useful volume with reasonable acquisition cost?
3. What could an allocation produce if observed CAC remained constant?
4. How do customer value and delivery costs affect estimated contribution and break-even CAC?

Success means correct, explainable calculations and a defensible recommendation. It does not mean claiming a real revenue increase.

## Our collaboration

I implemented the initial technical draft. Your next checkpoint is to open the demo, choose a campaign, and explain its spend, leads and customers in your own words. Then compare two channels and propose one question you would ask the marketing manager before changing their budget. We will review that reasoning before writing the final recommendation.

Learn in order: business question → table relationships → hand-calculated measures → SQL → dashboard interpretation → scenario assumptions → management presentation. You do not need to memorize the application code.
